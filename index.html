Qibla Compass – Mecca Direction Privacy Policy

Qibla Compass – Mecca Direction respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

This app uses your device’s location only to calculate the direction of the Qibla relative to your current position. The location data is processed locally on your device and is never stored, collected, or transmitted to any external server.

Data Collection

The app does not collect personal information, does not track users, and does not store location data.

In-App Purchases

The app includes optional one-time tip purchases that allow users to support development. These purchases are processed securely through Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive or store payment information.

Analytics

This app does not use analytics, tracking tools, or third-party data collection services.

Advertising

This app does not contain advertisements.

Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future if the app’s features change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact: support@praymate.app
